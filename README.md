# LinkedIn Talent Mapping and Sourcing Analysis
### Excel | LinkedIn | Naukri | Internshala | 2025

---

## Project Summary

A structured talent mapping exercise researching 40 candidate profiles
for the Data Analyst and Business Analyst role across Delhi NCR and
Bengaluru. Simulates the end-to-end sourcing workflow a Talent
Acquisition team runs before opening a role — identifying the right
platforms, search strategies, candidate profile patterns, and
sourcing gaps.

---

## Workbook Structure

### Sheet 1 — Candidate Profiles (Raw Data)
13-column candidate tracker capturing:
- Candidate ID, Full Name, Current Role, Current Company
- City, Experience (Years), Education, Key Skills
- Source Platform, Notice Period, Profile Score (1–5)
- Shortlist Status (Shortlisted / Hold / Rejected), Remarks

40 profiles sourced across:
- LinkedIn: 28 profiles
- Naukri: 8 profiles
- Internshala: 4 profiles

### Sheet 2 — Talent Analysis Dashboard
- KPI Summary: Total Sourced, Shortlisted, On Hold, Rejected,
  Shortlist Rate (70%), Avg Profile Score (3.8/5), Avg Notice (35 days)
- Source Platform Analysis: Profiles Found vs Shortlisted per channel
- Experience Level Split: 0–1 yr / 1–2 yr / 2–3 yr / 3+ yr
- Top Skills Frequency: SQL (90%), Excel (95%), Power BI (65%)
- Education Background distribution
- Charts: Bar (platform comparison), Pie (experience split)

### Sheet 3 — Sourcing Recommendations Report
- Executive Summary
- Sourcing Channel Strategy (with Boolean search strings)
- Ideal Candidate Profile based on data
- Talent Gaps Identified
- 4-Step Outreach and Follow-up Action Plan

---

## Key Findings

- LinkedIn is the highest-converting sourcing channel at 75%
- The 2–2.9 years experience band has the largest talent supply (40%)
- SQL is present in 90% of shortlisted profiles — non-negotiable skill
- Machine Learning exposure: only 20% of profiles — avoid making it mandatory
- Internshala: 100% fresher profiles, ideal for zero notice period urgent hiring
- Bengaluru has strong quality profiles but relocation must be confirmed upfront

---

## Profile Scoring Rubric (1–5)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Top analytics firm (Mu Sigma, Fractal, Zomato) + strong skill stack |
| 4 | Good skill match, relevant role, manageable notice period |
| 3 | Partial match or location concern — Hold |
| 2 | Overqualified or poor skill fit — Reject |
| 1 | Completely different domain — Reject |

## Boolean Search Strings Used

LinkedIn People Search:
"Data Analyst" OR "Business Analyst" AND "SQL" AND ("Noida" OR "Delhi" OR "Gurugram")
"BI Analyst" OR "Data Analyst" AND "Power BI" AND "Bengaluru"
"Business Analyst" AND "SQL" AND "MBA" AND ("Delhi" OR "NCR")
